# Industry analysis with counterevidence

Define audience, research scope and actual data cutoff. Map the value chain and distinguish segment-level demand, supply, competition, capex and policy. Ground numerical claims in dated original sources and look for contrary indicators. Future end-of-year statements before that date are scenarios, not observations. Explain causal investment implications, valuation uncertainty, downside cases and what evidence would change the judgment. Preserve source/claim ledgers for independent review.
